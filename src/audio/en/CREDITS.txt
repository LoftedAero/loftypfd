Lofty PFD's voice clips (*.wav here) are spoken by Piper's "cori" voice
(en_GB-cori-medium, https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices), made with
tools/voice.py.

The voice is that of Cori Samuel, a volunteer reader for LibriVox
(https://librivox.org). It was trained on the Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS
Dataset (reader 92) by Evelina Bakhturina, Vitaly Lavrukhin, Boris Ginsburg
and Yang Zhang, NVIDIA Corporation, https://www.openslr.org/109/, licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The clips are trimmed, resampled to 16 kHz and leveled from what the voice
produced.
